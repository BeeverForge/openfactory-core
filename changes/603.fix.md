**The product role acts on a requirement however the person says it.** Agreeing to a requirement, dropping it, writing a decision into it and noting a fact were reached only by fixed sentences such as "aceita o requisito 66". "pode aceitar o 66" or "sim, concordo com o requisito 66" reached nothing, and the role, which had understood, had no way to act. The role now proposes each of the four from what it understood, and the person confirms it as before; a drop's question shows the reason that will be written in their name. A fixed sentence no longer acts by itself.
