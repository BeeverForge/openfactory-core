**The address decides which page opens, not the credential.** `/p/<project>/board` is the board and `/product/<project>` is the product owner's page, whoever opens them. The product owner reaches the board read only, from their page's Board tab. A board credential that opens the product owner's page is told it is refused, and given the way to its board. A page whose credential changes under it in another tab opens again as the page that credential may see.
