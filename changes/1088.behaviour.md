**An `ack` or a `refused` on the product chat's socket says which product it answers about.** When it answers about a product the credential may read — a message sent into the subscribed conversation, a conversation that was refused, a message that broke on the server — the frame carries that product's key as `product`, so a page following several products on one socket can tell its answers apart; an answer about no product, or to a credential that may not read the product area, carries none.
