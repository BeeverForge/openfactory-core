**A product can declare its own requirement template.** `template.yaml` beside the requirements lists the company's header fields (text, choice, date; required; listed) and sections; the corpus reads them into `Requirement.fields` and `.sections`, a requirement that does not meet the template is a warning (`field-missing`, `field-value`, `section-missing`), `propose_requirement` writes them, and the role's index shows the `listed` fields. The core's own header lines and sections cannot be taken, and a template that cannot be used is a `template-refused` warning with the core's shape applied. A template holds at most 50 values of 60 characters to a choice and 300 characters of `help`, beside its 30 fields, 20 sections and names of 60 characters, and one past any bound is a `template-refused` warning that names it. Also fixed: a header field is matched by its whole label, so `- **Date of go-live:**` above `- **Date:**` is no longer read as the date. ADR-0058.
