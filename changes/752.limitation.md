**Under a Jira `scope_jql`, the tickets the factory creates must match the scope by other means.** Its create writes only the project, summary, description and issue type, so a split's children, the product role's cards and its impediments carry no label of the scope: outside it, the poller does not pick them up, the panel and the product role do not show them, and the search for an earlier filing does not find them, so a retry files another. Scope by something every new issue of the project receives, such as a label or component a Jira automation rule sets on each issue the project's own account creates; `docs/reference/configuration.md` says how.
