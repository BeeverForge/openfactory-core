**Pausing pickup, naming and declaring a product, revoking a person and running the doctor are actions.** A front end of one's own reaches each with `POST /api/act/{name}` — `poller_pause`, `poller_resume`, `product_name`, `product_declare`, `people_revoke` and `doctor`, whose data is the `openfactory.doctor/1` document — and the command line's verbs map onto the same rows, with their flags and their success output unchanged — `poller pause` and `poller resume` now print a refusal on stderr, where the missing-engine one already went.
- `openfactory people revoke <id>` is new: it ends a person's registration, every session they hold and any invitation still open for them, records who did it, and refuses the last person registered while no credential closes the panel's door.
- Changing the installation is the administrator's: a credential scoped to the board or to the product is refused these rows and `people_invite` with a 403.
