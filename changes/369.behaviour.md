**The product role's next turn inside a minute asks no forge about the product's sources, and an operator sets how many are brought up at once.** A source brought up less than `OPENFACTORY_SOURCES_FRESH_SECONDS` ago (60 when unset; `0` asks every turn) is served as it was, so a change pushed in that window reaches the role on the first turn after it; `OPENFACTORY_SOURCES_PARALLEL` (8 when unset) sets how many of a product's sources are brought up side by side, for a turn and for the system layer's refresh. A cache carried across the upgrade has no window yet, so its first turn asks every forge as before.
