**What the automatic review found reaches a person in their own language.** When a card from a requirement's breakdown did not pass the automatic review, the conversation and the owner's page said what was missing in the review's English instructions to the drafter, inside a Portuguese sentence: "O que falta: Name the reservations screen …". A card shown for a yes while the review still disputed it quoted the same English. A person now reads the review's findings in the project's language. The review writes a line for the person beside the ones it writes for the redraft, and the platform's own checks have a sentence in each language. When the review gave no such line, the person is told so in their language. The redraft still receives the findings as they are.
