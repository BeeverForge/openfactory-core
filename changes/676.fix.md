**A proposal tells each conflict for what it is.** Every conflict the product owner raised before a yes was framed as going against something already decided, "a change of mind, not an addition", whatever the drafter had recorded. A date format that only added detail to an agreed requirement was told to the person as a change of mind. A contradiction is still told that way; a request that repeats, adds detail to or builds on an agreed requirement is now told in its own words, and a proposal with several kinds of conflict says each under its own.
