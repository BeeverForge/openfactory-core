**A product refusal answers as a refusal, not as an internal error.** When the product role declines a write on purpose — the card is not yours to withdraw, it is already closed, the requirement does not exist — the action now answers 403, 409, 404 or 400 with the role's own sentence and the role's reason in `data.refusal`, where it answered 500; a write that was tried and did not happen is still a 500, and carries in `data.reference` the reference its log line names.
