**A project's floor no longer says its next card will be picked up while every job slot of the deployment is held, and a parked job says what it holds.** A job parked for a person still holds its slot, and the floor counted only its own project's jobs. With the default single slot, a park on one project held every other project's cards for hours while their floors said "Armed — the next card will be picked up", and the product owner could not say why To Do did not move. A floor now says the next card waits for a slot, without naming another project's job, and says what frees it. The product owner is told that a live job holds a slot and that an impediment's deadline sets the card aside rather than waking it. `merge`, `adjust` and `discard` asked of a parked job now say to answer it with `resume` or `skip`.
