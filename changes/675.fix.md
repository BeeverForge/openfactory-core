**The worker's and the panel's logs carry a time, a level and a source on every line, and keep the platform's INFO lines.** Neither process set its log up, so a warning reached the log as its bare message, with no time, level or source, and every INFO line was dropped: a context push rejected twice showed one line, and failures could be placed in time only from the engine's own history. Every line is now `<UTC time> <LEVEL> <logger>: <message>`, the panel's request lines included, and `OPENFACTORY_LOG_LEVEL` sets the platform's level (`INFO` when unset). Libraries stay at `WARNING`.
