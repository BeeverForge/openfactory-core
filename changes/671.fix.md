**An impediment the factory files on a person's own board is titled in the project's language.** On a deployment where the factory's own cards land on the product's board — every local installation, and a factory board declared on the product's own tracker — an impediment was titled in English beside a Portuguese team's own cards, such as "[openfactory] reservas-web: the product's requirements cannot be read". It is now titled in the project's language there. A factory board of its own keeps the one spelling it always had. An impediment already open under either spelling is still recognised: it is not filed again when the language changes, and it closes when the capability works again.
