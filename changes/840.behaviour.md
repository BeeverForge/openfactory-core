**A project's model, language, preview policy and removal are actions.** A front end of one's own changes them with `POST /api/act/{name}` — `project_set_model`, `project_set_language`, `project_set_preview` and `project_remove` — and `openfactory project set-model`, `set-language`, `set-preview` and `remove` map onto the same rows with their flags, output and exit codes unchanged; like every row that changes the installation, they are refused to a credential scoped to the board or the product.
