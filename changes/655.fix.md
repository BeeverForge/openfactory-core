**The product owner answers a person in the language they wrote in.** On a project set to `pt-BR`, an English question was answered in Portuguese. Every harness already told the model to reply in the writer's language, but the product owner's own prompt then said "Write in BRAZILIAN Portuguese … the reader is Brazilian", and the more specific rule won. Its language rule now says first to answer in the language the person wrote in. The dialect rule still governs what the product owner says first and its replies to anyone who writes in the project's language. A card drafted in the conversation's language keeps the dialect rule alone, because a card is not a reply.
