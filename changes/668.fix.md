**A card the factory has taken up says why it offers no Send back.** On the owner's page, a card waiting at the human merge gate could show neither the Send back button nor any sentence, when the engine had not yet published its gate or could not be reached. Whoever opened it was left guessing. The card now always says why: the change is not waiting on them yet, the engine could not be asked, the passes are spent, or only the person who asked for it or an approver can send it back. Reading an engine error as "no job ever ran for this card" is now written in the log.
