**A late yes typed in a project's room is told that its proposal expired, and nobody is asked a question no yes of theirs can answer.** The panel writes `@po` in front of every message typed in a room, and the platform read "@po sim" as neither a yes nor a no, so a person who confirmed after a proposal expired was never told, and the product role proposed the same thing again in their name. A yes or a no is now read without the mention that addressed it. And where the person asking may not act and no admin may confirm on their behalf, every act the role proposes is refused at once, naming who can: agreeing to a requirement, dropping it, recording a decision, noting a fact, closing, correcting, retitling or aligning a card, refining it and breaking a requirement down. Before, only the breakdown and the refinement were refused; the others waited for a yes that could never come. Where an admin may confirm, an agreement now names them under the question, as every other act did.
