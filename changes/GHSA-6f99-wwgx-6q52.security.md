**A shared board or product token keeps its scope however it is presented.** `OPENFACTORY_BOARD_TOKEN` and `OPENFACTORY_PRODUCT_TOKEN` now act only in their own area whether or not a request sends an `X-OpenFactory-Actor` label, and whether the token comes in the `Authorization` header, the credential cookie or `?token=`; a request without the label, or a read presenting the token by cookie or query, was treated as the unscoped panel token. Details in the advisory.
