**Requirements written elsewhere can be imported into a product, under the corpus's rules.** `openfactory act product_import` (and `POST /api/act/product_import`) takes a folder or an archive of requirements, facts and documents, shows the plan with a token, and lands it on `yes` in one commit pushed once under the product's semaphore. Every requirement is numbered after the highest number the product ever used and after any proposal still on its way, with references renumbered; the same title is the same promise — the same text adds only its missing decision rows, a new text revises a requirement nobody agreed, and a new text for an accepted one — or one whose own history reaches an accepted one — is kept in the import's record under `.openfactory/imports/<id>/` without touching the promise; the corpus is checked before the commit, and an import that would break it or change an agreed requirement lands nothing. Nothing lands as accepted, a known fact is never rewritten, documents land under `imported/` and never over an existing file, and the product's knowledge is refreshed afterwards. Only a product admin may import; from anywhere but the command line, the folder must be in the product's import inbox.
