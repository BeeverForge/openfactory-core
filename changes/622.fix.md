**Bringing a pull request up to date on the local forge no longer switches your branch.** When a job's branch fell behind, the factory rebased it in your own working tree, which left you on `openfactory/<n>` afterwards, and failed outright if you had uncommitted changes. It now rebases in a temporary tree of its own, so your current branch, your index and your uncommitted changes are left as they were, your `rebase.updateRefs` setting moves none of your branches, and the registrations of your own linked worktrees are never pruned. The rebased commits name the factory's bot as their committer.
