**The product role knows when a requirement is already being broken into tasks, and a breakdown started from the panel is told in the conversation the requirement came from.** For each agreed requirement, the role now reads whether its breakdown is running and which cards already carry its work, so it points there or offers the breakdown instead of proposing a separate card. Accepting a requirement, or asking for its breakdown, from the panel, the command line or the API now also tells the conversation the requirement was asked in what was filed.
