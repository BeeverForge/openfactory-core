**`openfactory bot-token` no longer accepts a `--key-path` it never read.** The flag was accepted and ignored, and the command minted with the App key the deployment holds; it is gone, and `--help` says the key comes through the credential store, as `OPENFACTORY_GH_APP_KEY_CONTENT` or `OPENFACTORY_GH_APP_KEY`, from the store `OPENFACTORY_CREDENTIAL_STORE` declares and then from the environment.
