**A requirement's breakdown no longer files work its own cards already carry, or work filed while it ran.** The breakdown now reads the cards that already execute the requirement, open and closed, in their own words, and drafts only what they do not carry: a delivered requirement does not get its work filed again. Just before each card, it asks the board again, and a card of the requirement opened in the meantime holds the rest of the breakdown, which names that card.
