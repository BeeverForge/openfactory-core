**Several projects can share one Jira project.** A Jira tracker takes `scope_jql`, a JQL condition such as `labels = "of-api"`, and the project's pickup queue, its board as the panel and the product role read it, and the search for an already filed ticket of the same title are limited to the issues it selects, so one project no longer picks up or reuses another's tickets. The condition is ANDed in parentheses, so an `OR` inside it cannot widen the read past the project; a condition Jira refuses reads as an unreadable board and the warning names it. A scoped project refuses, by name, to edit, close, remove or queue a card outside its slice, which is another project's to act on. Unset, the whole Jira project is read as before.
