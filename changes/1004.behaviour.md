**A product's knowledge can be brought up to date now, without holding a request open.** `openfactory act product_refresh --param project=<name>` (`POST /api/act/product_refresh`) reads what changed in the product's context repository as the scheduled pass does, then brings the product's search index up to those documents, the requirements and the board of every registry project of the product. It answers at once with `refresh: started`, or `joined` when a refresh of that product is already running, and the work runs on the worker, so it needs the durable engine; with none it is refused as unavailable. `product_status` now answers `knowledge`: when the index was last brought up to date, how the last refresh ended (`refreshed`, `degraded`, `failed` or `no-context`), whether the schedule or a person asked for it, each thing it did not bring up, and whether one is running. `product_ingest` is unchanged and still answers inside the request.
