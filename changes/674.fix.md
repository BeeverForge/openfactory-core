**An engine that does not answer in time is a 503 that says so, from every route of the panel and every action, never a 500 with a traceback.** `/api/inbox`, `/api/coordinator/messages` and `/api/decisions` caught only a failed connection, so a read that ran out of time escaped them as a 500 and a ~200-line traceback in the log: 103 of them in one half hour of a long test run. An action whose read ran out of time, such as `diagnose`, answered 500 too. Every route and every action now answers 503 with the read's own sentence, and the log carries one line for it.
