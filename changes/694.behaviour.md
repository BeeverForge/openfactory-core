**Every action leaves a durable record of who asked for it, and every refusal does too.** Each call — from the panel, the command line or a chat — is written to the metrics store with the person, the identity provider, the transport, the action, its parameters with secrets masked and a client's words kept out, the outcome and its reason, the project and a correlation id, and is kept for 365 days; `openfactory audit` lists them by person, action, project, outcome and period. The panel takes the correlation id from the `X-Request-ID` header and the command line makes one, and a job an action starts carries it in its events. The action's INFO log line prints its parameters as the record keeps them, so a client's words appear there as their length and digest too, no longer as text.
