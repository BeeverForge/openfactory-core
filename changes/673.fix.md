**The members of one product publish their knowledge maps one after another, and a publish no longer makes a person's save fail.** The projects of one product refresh their knowledge on one clock, so their module maps were published into the shared context repository at the same second. The publish took no lock and retried once: with three members, the third map was rejected twice and dropped until the next refresh. The same publish could also land between a person's clone and their push of a requirement, a decision or a fact, and those writes push only once, so the person's save failed. Every publish of derived knowledge now holds the product's semaphore around its clone, its commit and its push, as the system layer's already did. A publish that cannot have the semaphore in time publishes nothing, logs it, and leaves it to the next refresh. `openfactory forget --with-context` now holds the semaphore too when it removes the conversations' distillates.
