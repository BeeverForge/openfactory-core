**The product role says where its requirements came from.** An import that lands is told once to the product's room — what arrived, from where, and how many requirements were added, revised or kept beside an agreed one, naming no person. While it is a review to hold, the briefing carries a line read from the newest import's record (its origin, how many are still proposed and agreed, where the design is), and what is still open — every imported requirement's open questions, the questions the import brought, the decisions its documents raise and every text kept beside an agreed requirement — is on the agenda as items to ask, closing as the requirements are accepted, dropped or replaced. The role's instructions treat an import as a review with the person who asked, and the evaluation battery has a `saltmarsh` fixture whose question is the conversation that opens after an import.
