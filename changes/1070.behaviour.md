**A preview comes down without docker's grace period.** Ending a preview now gives each of its containers 2 seconds to stop before it is killed, where docker granted 10, so a preview whose processes ignore SIGTERM, as a process run as PID 1 with no init does, comes down in seconds rather than tens of seconds per container. Nothing is lost by the shorter wait: the same `down` deletes the preview's volumes and the images it built. An operator who wants the grace back sets `stop_seconds` on the compose runtime row.
