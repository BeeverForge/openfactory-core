**A product's knowledge is searchable through the front-end API.** `GET /api/product/<project>/search?q=…&limit=…` searches one product's requirements, the decisions recorded in its documents, the documents themselves and its board's cards — the kinds a product publishes — in the same hybrid ranking the product owner's turns use (lexical, then semantic, an exact-term tier first). Each hit carries its kind, its source, its product, a snippet and its rank within that product; a line of a conversation and a model's distillate of one are never returned. A product whose semantic stage is degraded says so in the answer, the read runs under no product semaphore and records nothing, and a rank is a rank within one product — the raw score is never presented as comparable across products.
