**A proposal adjusted before the yes keeps what the person asked for.** The product role wrote a requirement draft from the person's last message alone. So when someone asked for a CSV export filtered in the API and in the client's currency, and then added "filter by status too", the draft that replaced it was a filter on the screen's list, while the reply above it said the rest of the proposal was unchanged; the export and the currency were in no requirement. Every draft — a requirement, a card a person asks for, a defect — is now written from the conversation, the role's reply, and the draft it replaces, whether that draft is still waiting for the yes or the person's "no" just took it off the table; the drafter is told to keep everything the person did not change.
